# Pokémon VGC doubles draft: team playbook (edit me)

Sent with every Pokémon decision (draft pick, team preview, battle turn). Plain
English; change the strategy here, no code needed. Keep it short.

## Draft (6 picks from a shared 18-card pool, snake order)

- Draft a team, not six attackers. By the end you want:
  - **Speed control**: a Tailwind setter (Tornadus, Whimsicott, Talonflame...)
    or Trick Room (Farigiraf, Hatterene, Cresselia...), and Icy Wind /
    Electroweb / Thunder Wave users. In test games the opponent took speed
    control every time and we usually had none: take one early.
  - **Fake Out** (Incineroar, Rillaboom, Iron Hands...) and **Intimidate**
    (Incineroar, Landorus-Therian, Gyarados, Arcanine...).
  - **Two or three strong attackers** that cover each other's weaknesses, at
    least one hitting hard on each side (physical and special).
  - **Redirection or disruption** if offered (Amoonguss Rage Powder, screens,
    Taunt) and a way to handle Fairy, Dragon and Steel types.
- First picks: if a top threat is in the pool (Flutter Mane, Urshifu,
  Chien-Pao...), take it before Incineroar: in test games we took Incineroar
  first every time and the opponent's Flutter Mane + Urshifu lead knocked out
  both our leads within two turns, twice.
- Later picks: fill the role you're missing, and deny the opponent the one
  piece their team obviously needs (their only speed control, their counter
  to your main attacker).
- Avoid a team that shares one weakness (three Pokémon weak to Ground, etc.).

## Team preview (bring 4 of 6, lead 2)

- Look at the opponent's six. Bring the four that beat their likely four:
  your speed control, your Fake Out user and your best two attackers for
  this matchup.
- Lead with Fake Out + a strong attacker, or with your speed control +
  attacker so Tailwind / Trick Room goes up on turn 1. If your team is slow and
  built around Trick Room, **lead the Trick Room setter**: in testing we kept
  Farigiraf / Cresselia in the back and set speed control only after both
  leads had fainted.
- Keep a Pokémon in the back that handles their scariest threat.

## Battle turns

- Type immunities before choosing targets: **Ghost types (Flutter Mane,
  Gholdengo, Sableye...) are immune to Fake Out and other Normal moves and to
  Fighting moves**; Flying types and Levitate to Ground moves; Dark types to
  Prankster status moves. We Fake Out'd Flutter Mane in two test games and
  wasted the turn.
- Turn 1: Fake Out the most dangerous opponent (or their speed-control
  setter) while your partner sets Tailwind / Trick Room or attacks.
- Focus fire: two attacks on one target usually beat one hit on each. Knock
  out the biggest threat first.
- Protect when a double target on you is obvious, when your partner can win
  the turn alone, or to stall out the opponent's Tailwind / Trick Room turns.
  Don't Protect twice in a row (it likely fails).
- Spread moves (Earthquake, Rock Slide, Heat Wave, Dazzling Gleam) are strong
  when they hit two targets; don't Earthquake your own partner unless it's
  immune (Flying, Levitate).
- Switch out a Pokémon that is about to be knocked out for nothing, or to
  reset Intimidate / Fake Out; otherwise keep attacking.
- Count speed: under your Tailwind you usually move first; under their Trick
  Room the slowest moves first.
